# What this is

One or two lines. Assume the reader is competent and has no context on this
particular routine.

# Steps

1. The first thing to do. Concrete, in plain words, like you would tell a
   colleague who is good at their job but has never done this specific task.
2. The next thing. Say what "done" looks like, not just what to run.
3. Keep going. Numbered steps, in order, no branching unless the branch is real.

# Rules

- The things that must always be true. One line each.
- The thing you learned the hard way and would otherwise forget by next month.
- What NOT to do, but only where it is genuinely tempting to do it.

# Done when

The finish line, stated so it can be checked rather than felt. If you cannot
write this line, the routine is not ready to be a skill yet. It is still a
conversation.
